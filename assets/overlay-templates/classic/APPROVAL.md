# Classic v1 — experimental showcase

**Status: experimental, NOT founder-approved.** Requested by the Darve owner on 2026-11-23 to showcase and test immersive OBS cards (three.js shaders with GSAP animation). It must not be priced, offered to creators or activated until a founder approval record replaces this file.

Original project-authored GLSL, CSS and TypeScript in this repository. No external artwork, third-party template, remote font or downloaded script is included. three.js and GSAP are existing pinned npm dependencies under their own licences. Inter keeps its bundled font licence; Georgia/Times are system fonts and are not shipped.

Review: the WebGL canvas only decorates. Challenge text, credits, verified funding, declared completion and the conditional public contribution QR stay in the unchanged safe card DOM above it. The QR stays black on white. Reduced motion renders a still frame, a missing WebGL context leaves the CSS backdrop, and hidden documents stop rendering. Exact id/version/renderer/hash must match the local registry; missing, unknown or withdrawn metadata hides the display.
