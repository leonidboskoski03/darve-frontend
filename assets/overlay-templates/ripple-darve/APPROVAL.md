**Status: experimental, NOT founder-approved.** Requested by the Darve owner on 2026-11-23: colour variants for every OBS template with one shared layout. This is Ripple (one soft ring per verified contribution) in Darve brand pink and magenta with the Darve logo. It must not be priced, offered to creators or activated until a founder approval record replaces this file.

Original project-authored GLSL, CSS and TypeScript in this repository. No external artwork, third-party template, remote font or downloaded script is included. three.js and GSAP are existing pinned npm dependencies under their own licences; Inter keeps its bundled font licence; the Darve logo is Darve's own asset. Platform variants use colours only and no platform logos or trademarks.

Review: same shared card layout as every immersive template; calm motion, no flashing. The WebGL canvas only decorates; challenge text, credits, verified funding, declared completion and the conditional QR stay in the unchanged safe card DOM, and the QR stays black on white. Reduced motion renders a still frame, missing WebGL leaves the CSS backdrop, hidden documents stop rendering. Exact id/version/renderer/hash must match the local registry.
