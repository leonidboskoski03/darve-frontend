# Darve policy draft review

These English-language drafts are for F03-G02/D02 review. The operator and legal reviewer should resolve bracketed text and approve the final scope and wording before publishing or requesting acceptance.

- [Terms of Service](TERMS_OF_SERVICE_DRAFT.md)
- [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY_DRAFT.md)
- [Community and Safety Guidelines](COMMUNITY_SAFETY_DRAFT.md)

| Decision                     | Verify before release                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Operator and audience        | Legal entity, address, support and privacy contacts, applicable markets and languages, creator/viewer/guest/staff scope, and any required local representative or privacy contact.                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Age and eligibility          | Minimum ages and guardian process for viewing, chat submissions, creator accounts, guest checkout and receiving funds. Reconcile each with launch countries and streaming/payment provider requirements.                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Payment terms                | Confirm merchant/seller roles, fee agreements and checkout disclosures, taxes, payouts, surplus, failed or missed goals, late payments, refund eligibility, who performs a refund, and dispute/chargeback support. Match the on-screen terms and actual enabled payment methods.                                                                                                |
| Content, safety and disputes | Confirm content permissions and attribution, reporting and guest access to support, moderation/appeal actions and notices, closure effects, governing law and any dispute or liability terms. Confirm any required platform or statutory notices with counsel.                                                                                                                  |
| Personal data and vendors    | Inventory actual data fields, sources and recipients for each feature. Approve lawful bases, cookies and durations, identity/mail and AI vendors, AI payload and provider settings, storage regions and transfer safeguards. Check the launch configuration before describing optional features.                                                                                |
| Retention and rights         | Confirm retention for chat and derived data, grants, payments, receipts, tickets, staff audit, rewards, logs and backups. Document rights intake, identity verification, exceptions, deletion and backup restore behavior. Review local and launch-region notice requirements.                                                                                                  |
| Rewards and templates        | Decide which features launch and approve program terms for eligibility, redemption, correction, expiry, restrictions and treatment of existing entitlements.                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Publication and consent      | Assign an effective date and immutable version to each approved text. Provide signed-out reading and link notices from creator setup, relevant collection surfaces and checkout as applicable. Match the creator acceptance record to the exact text/version shown. Preserve earlier accepted versions and keep provider permissions and any separate privacy choices distinct. |
