# Darve Community and Safety Guidelines

**Draft for review.** Effective date and version are pending. These guidelines accompany the [Terms of Service](TERMS_OF_SERVICE_DRAFT.md).

## 1. Everyone has a choice

Viewers can suggest challenges. Creators choose whether to accept or perform them and can stop if circumstances change. A contribution does not entitle a viewer to demand unsafe conduct or pressure anyone to continue. Creators should check the rules of their streaming platform, game, venue and activity and get permission from people who would take part or appear on stream.

## 2. What is prohibited

- Challenges that encourage injury, self-harm, dangerous substance use, dangerous driving or stunts, or unsafe handling of weapons.
- Illegal activity, fraud, theft, property damage, unauthorized access or deceptive fundraising.
- Threats, targeted harassment, hate, degrading treatment, coercion or sexual exploitation.
- Sexual content involving minors, attempts to solicit private contact with minors, or challenges that put minors at risk.
- Sharing someone’s private information, credentials, private OBS or receipt links, or images or recordings without appropriate permission.
- Impersonation, spam, manipulated contributions, and attempts to bypass safety or moderation controls.

## 3. Before accepting a challenge

Check the wording, participants, location, equipment and time limits. Consider who could be harmed and whether everyone affected can freely decline. Decline or stop if the activity becomes unsafe, consent changes, or you cannot follow platform rules. If there is immediate danger, stop the activity and contact local emergency services when appropriate.
