All listed success responses are HTTP 200. Configuration/account direct roots have `schemaVersion` but no `data` or `serverTime`; direct command responses have `schemaVersion,data` but no `serverTime`. Pagination accepts only optional `cursor` (maximum 4096 characters), not client page size, offset or owner overrides. Do not invent coin SSE, a mission-claim endpoint, coin purchases, asset installation or wallet transfer APIs.

### Exact state and command behavior

`CoinAmountSchema` is a canonical nonnegative integer decimal string bounded by `9223372036854775807`. Ledger deltas are signed nonzero strings. Preserve strings and use checked `BigInt` only when calculation is needed; never use floating point or imply a cash exchange rate. A null spendable balance/reward/price means unknown or unavailable, not zero/free. A configured zero template price is different from a missing price revision.

Account `unconfigured`, `unavailable`, `synchronization_pending` and `synchronized` are distinct. Display balance separately from spendable balance and honor `redemptionAllowed`. Mission rewards and earned amounts can be null. Verify the six unique mission IDs and state relationships rather than relying solely on array length. Catalogue `owned` and `ownershipState` must not be collapsed with `available`.

Redemption reviews exact `{expectedAccountRevision,priceRevision,amount,confirmation:{templateId}}`. For example, if the reviewed price changes before submission, refetch the same template/account, show the new price and require a fresh unchecked confirmation before creating a new command. A timeout preserves the original key/body and held result; retry that exact command or reconcile canonical reads. Never create another redemption merely to resolve uncertainty. Disable duplicate submission while a request is in flight.

A redemption response reports `already_owned` or `synchronization_pending`, not a new confirmed usable purchase. Reconcile account, ledger and entitlements after the durable journal consumer catches up; do not optimistically debit or animate an unlock as proof. Selection submits `{expectedRevision,templateId}` from current template preferences, whose revision is independent of overlay layout/display versions. On a stale/unknown selection result read canonical preferences and require a new decision if the desired selection remains unapplied; do not invent replay semantics. Logout, account switch, expired authority and staff reassignment cancel reads and clear scoped caches/reviewed commands.
