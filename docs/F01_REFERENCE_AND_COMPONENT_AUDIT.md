Creator, staff, guest receipt and overlay authorities remain independent. Long text, all grouped credits, large exact amounts, mixed currencies and keyboard/reduced-motion/mobile use must be represented in the later component/feature acceptance.

Resend stays server-side. No extra creator password/login flow is inferred from old marketing forms. Staff code delivery and provider/sandbox prerequisites remain explicit acceptance gaps under their existing owners.
